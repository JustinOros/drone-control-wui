Controls
Click the map to add waypoints, click a waypoint to remove it.
Manual mode: W/S forward and back, A/D turn left and right, Q/E strafe sideways, Space up, Left Shift down.
Xbox (default layout, change it with the gear icon): left stick climb, descend and yaw, right stick move. Y takeoff, X land, Back return to launch, Start pause or play the mission, L3 manual on or off, LB/RB speed, A picture, B record.
